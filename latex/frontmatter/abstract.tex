\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Banks document REST APIs with OpenAPI contracts; BIAN describes business capabilities through
Service Domains, behaviors and business objects. Teams often maintain both layers without a
repeatable check that the technical contract matches the reference business model. This thesis
plan (Group 9.2, Guide~01) specifies a measurement procedure: SemanticScore (S), StructuralScore
(E), CoverageScore (C) and an aggregated AlignmentScore, traced in the consistency matrix
(Appendix~C). Scope is limited to documentary artifacts (OpenAPI contract plus BIAN excerpt); we
do not implement the tool in production nor assess live API security (§1.6).
