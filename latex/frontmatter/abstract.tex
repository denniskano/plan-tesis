\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Banks document REST APIs with OpenAPI contracts. BIAN describes business capabilities through
Service Domains, behaviors and business objects. Teams maintain both layers without a repeatable
check that the technical contract matches the reference business model before integration.

This thesis plan specifies a measurement procedure: SemanticScore (S), StructuralScore (E),
CoverageScore (C) and an aggregated AlignmentScore, traced in the consistency matrix (Appendix~C).
Scope is limited to documentary artifacts (OpenAPI contract plus BIAN excerpt). We do not
implement the tool in production nor assess live API security (§\ref{sec:alcances}).
